## Summary

- **23 test cases executed** — 12 Pass, 11 Fail.
- **Highest priority, by a wide margin — TC-23.** Payment completion appears to trust a client-controlled redirect URL (`/Payment/Complete?...&StatusMessage=Success`) rather than an independent server-to-server check with the card processor. A non-Luhn-valid, fake card number was "approved" and produced a full payment receipt. This is the likely root cause of TC-21 (orders that skip the card form and go straight to "paid"), and — if confirmed — means order payment status may be forgeable without a real card at all. Recommend this be treated as a P0 security issue, not routine QA feedback.
- **TC-21 + TC-22:** The Pay flow is inconsistent across orders: two orders (11408579, 11408568) skipped payment entirely; a third (11408602) showed a real card form, but with first-party (non-PCI-hosted-iframe) card fields.
- **Next priority:** TC-17 (View Invoice popup blocked) — breaks a core user-facing feature for most default browser configurations.
- **Security items worth prioritizing:** TC-11 (PII in URL query string) and TC-13 (tokens logged to console) — both are information-disclosure issues on a page that handles patient/order data.
- **Access control (TC-02/03/04) passed cleanly** — no evidence of data leakage between patients; both name and phone are genuinely required to match.
- **Injection testing (TC-09/10) passed** — client-side validation blocked both XSS- and SQLi-shaped payloads before submission.
